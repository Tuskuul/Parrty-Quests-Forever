Simply Party Quests v0.11.1 - WoW Forever (interface 16001)

INSTALL / UPDATE
Extract SimplyPartyQuests into the Forever client's Interface\AddOns folder.
The final path must be Interface\AddOns\SimplyPartyQuests\SimplyPartyQuests.toc.
Replace existing files, then use /reload if the game is running.
Existing saved settings are preserved. A first installation may need a client restart.

V0.7.0 PROGRESS SOURCE FALLBACKS
Restart the game after this update: QuestItemSources.lua is a new TOC entry.
- Remember real creature GUIDs from queried unit tokens even when the tooltip
  omits data.guid. A corpse can then remain a native progress source after it
  stops being targeted. Candidates still expire and use the bounded cache.
- 2,132 static QuestieDB quest-to-objective-item mappings seed item-tooltip
  queries for known quests. These include Arugal Must Die -> Head of Arugal
  (5442), and The Book of Ur -> The Book of Ur (6283). Static mappings identify
  candidate sources only; client text/counters remain the authority.
- Up to four rotating mapped item candidates per pass and up to four remembered
  sources run before direct party queries. No whole-item-database polling.
- Readable loot hyperlinks seed additional item candidates, including items you
  did not loot yourself. No recipient, quantity or loot notification is treated
  as quest progress. Only readable native quest/player/objective rows update it.
- Refresh also schedules a fresh background source pass. Dungeon polling works
  out of combat. A direct response can still be empty outside your own log;
  item/corpse replies can still be restricted or contain no party progress.
- Reports now include each known member quest's current progress, last source,
  latest direct-query outcome and mapped item-query status even with diagnostics
  off. This summary lasts for the current session; raw logging is still optional.
- Automated checks exercise an empty direct response followed by a readable
  Arugal item response, restricted replies, corpse retention and loot triggers.
  These checks do not prove the Forever client will expose the fallback data.
- This does not resolve escorts for which every native source only says On quest.

V0.6.1 DUNGEON POLLING FIX

  QUEST_TURNED_IN and PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED as refresh notifications.

  New notifications do not cancel active jobs or periodic membership validation.
- Work shares the existing three-request/0.15-second tick and soft time budget.
  Periodic work gets a slot after every two event requests when both are queued.
  Combat/instance guards still apply; events are captured and queued until the

- Reports retain recent events separately and label count changes with the
  triggering event batch. /spq diag on resets the event capture and counters.

V0.4.2 QUEST-REMOVAL FIX
- Known native quests are revalidated during background cycles, not just added
  by discovery. Quests absent on two separate validated passes (at least five
  seconds apart, no more than sixty) are removed by ID. Same-name chain steps
  are never merged or removed just because their titles match.
- Removal requires a connected, stable member, positive same-map or unit-
  visibility evidence, and at least one other quest that returns true in that
  same pass. Offline, different-zone, restricted, failed and all-false passes
  retain observations rather than pretending an unavailable log is empty.
- Ready quests stay listed until the membership API says they have left the
  log. Completing objectives alone is not a removal trigger.
- A stale mob/item/party tooltip cannot restore a removed quest until membership
  becomes positively true again. Reaccepted/repeatable quests can still return.
- Addon-to-addon full snapshots remain authoritative and can clear an empty log.

V0.4.1 REPORT-DRIVEN FIXES
- Native Ready for turn-in rows now set the quest's ready status. They no longer
  appear as an unnamed objective. Cached counts are retained internally without
  inventing completed amounts; the UI shows the confirmed ready status instead.
- Reports distinguish first observations, actual before/after counter changes,
  repeated identical counters, and readiness changes. Each change names its
  polling/hover source. Recent changes have a separate bounded history so a
  stream of repeated payloads cannot erase the evidence.
- Direct quest responses are counted separately for shared quests and quests
  absent from your current log. Your current quest IDs are included for comparison.
- Applied-row counters now count rows received rather than previously cached
  objectives carried forward by merging.
- An anonymized replay of the supplied live report passes automated checks.

V0.4.1 CHANGES
- Background progress polling now reads available target, focus, mouseover,
  party-member targets and visible nameplate unit tooltips without moving the
  mouse or changing the selected target.
- Up to 12 recently observed mob GUIDs / item IDs are re-queried through native

- Known quest party-progress queries have a dedicated parser for typed quest

WINDOW
Drag the title bar to move the window. Use - / + to collapse the whole window.
Click a party member's name to expand their comparison. Click either section
heading to collapse that entire list. Click a quest title to show or hide its
requirements and progress. New quests start collapsed; sections start open.
Position, visibility, dragging lock, and collapse states persist. Quest states
are per party member and follow the quest when it moves between sections.

Both on: quests currently in your log and the member's discovered/received list.
They're on, I'm not: their quests absent from your current log, including ones
you previously completed or abandoned. Your quests alone do not appear here.

REQUIREMENTS VERSUS PROGRESS
Quest requirements describe what the quest asks for, for example collecting
six items. These are reference instructions, not a report of the party member's
current inventory, kills or completion. English fallback summaries can differ
from the current client or be missing for newly added quests. Client-provided
text takes priority; missing cached data is requested asynchronously.

Progress unknown means no actual count has been observed for that quest yet.
Mouseover or target native quest tooltips can add the member's objective text
and counts. Different objectives can be observed at different times; each count
shows its own age. Only a fresh observation changes that age. Reference text
never marks a quest or objective complete. For shared quests your own progress
is displayed separately under You.

NATIVE DISCOVERY / POLLING (ONLY YOU NEED THIS ADDON)
The background sweep checks C_QuestLog.IsUnitOnQuest against 5,233 numeric
candidate IDs, plus your current quest IDs and previously discovered IDs. Only
positive membership matches cause title, requirement and progress requests.
The sweep starts after login/joining a party, repeats 120 seconds after
completion, and can be started with Refresh. Progress appears in the footer.
It processes at most 48 membership checks per 0.15-second tick and uses a
0.75ms soft time budget per batch when the profiling clock exists. A single
native API call can exceed that budget. Sweeps pause in combat and instances.
Solo players do no sweep, and recently full-synced members are skipped.

Native Unit, Object and Item tooltip rows are also consumed when present.
Ordinary item stats are never interpreted as party objective progress.

LIVE TEST FINDINGS SO FAR
The user confirmed native quest-name discovery and mouseover count updates.
Updates worked in the same zone in their test and did not work across different
zones. Zone-wide range has not been established. Automatic objective-count
refresh remains unconfirmed in the live client. V0.4.0 adds alternate native
tooltip queries and parsing; passing mocked responses does not prove the client
provides fresh counters through those calls.

A catalog sweep is a PARTIAL list, not proof of a complete remote quest log.
New IDs absent from the catalog, unavailable remote state, distance and
restricted data can all limit results. Negative checks alone do not remove prior observations. The guarded known-quest
validation described above confirms absence only when other remote quest state
is demonstrably available. If every remaining quest disappears at once, native
validation deliberately retains the last list because there is no positive
control quest; full addon sync can handle that empty-log case. Keep observation
ages in mind; /spq clear clears native-only data.

OPTIONAL FULL LOG SYNC (BOTH PLAYERS INSTALL)
Copies of this addon exchange quest snapshots through the invisible party addon
channel. Snapshots contain quest IDs, titles, readiness and objective text/counts.
No ordinary chat messages are posted, and inventory is not transmitted. A full
snapshot replaces a member's previous list, including quest removals and empty
logs. It takes priority over tooltip subsets for two minutes; stale snapshots
are marked awaiting an update. Cross-zone delivery still needs a live test.

Normal parties of up to five are supported, not raid quest comparisons.
Received/observed quest data is session-only. Leaving removes that member's data;
offline members keep their last list with an offline label. Demo data is separate.

FIRST V0.4.1 TEST
Join a party in the same zone, outdoors and out of combat. Type /spq diag on.
Pick one quest and observe its current count once, then move your mouse away.
Have the member advance it and wait around 10-20 seconds without hovering.
Try with a relevant target/nameplate available and then with neither available.
Check whether the count changes. If it does not, run /spq report and copy the
report. It includes quest IDs, party names and the raw readable payload fields;
review it before sharing. /spq diag off stops capture.

A background cycle finishes its queued work, then waits five seconds before the
next cycle. It processes at most three tooltip requests per 0.15-second tick,
with the same 0.75ms soft batch budget used by membership polling where the
profiling clock exists. Solo/demo states do no background work, and combat or
instances pause it. A long list can make a full cycle take longer than five
seconds. A native call itself can exceed the soft time budget.

If Blizzard supplies no objective counters, the addon keeps the last observed
values and timestamps instead of inventing an update. A timestamp is when data
was received; it cannot establish whether Blizzard's internal cache was fresh.
Diagnostic capture stores at most 350 bounded lines and starts off. Restricted
values are marked rather than read or converted to text. No report is sent
anywhere automatically.
